## Optimisation and replacement

- Forty photographs have local WebP variants at 480, 960 and 1440 pixels wide, quality 80. All 120 files together are approximately 8.47 MB. Responsive image selection downloads the appropriate variant for each viewport.
- The presence campaign removes unused space above the model; other sources retain their original proportions. Display crops are controlled by each section.
- `CampaignImage.svelte` sets intrinsic dimensions, responsive `srcset`/`sizes`, asynchronous decoding and lazy loading. The homepage hero and collection campaign images load eagerly with high priority; product grids load lazily. Alternate card photographs are requested on hover or keyboard focus.
- To replace an asset, export the three matching WebP widths to `static/images/<key>-<width>.webp`, update its source and dimensions in the manifest and review the alt text in `catalogue/products.ts`, `catalogue/collections.ts` and `content/home.ts`. Preserve the original aspect ratio across variants.
- Footwear selectors show real static photographic views. The two sneaker photographs and the two brogue photographs show matching shoes from their respective photographer series. There is no 3D viewer in this milestone.

## Typography

[Bodoni Moda](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bodoni+Moda) supplies normal and italic editorial type. [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans) supplies the 400, 500 and 600 interface weights. Fonts are self-hosted WOFF2 subsets with `font-display: swap` and Unicode ranges.

Both families use the SIL Open Font License. Original notices are retained in `apps/storefront/static/fonts/bodonimoda-OFL.txt` and `dmsans-OFL.txt`. Sources: [Bodoni Moda in Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bodonimoda) and [DM Sans in Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans).
